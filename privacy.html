Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy covers Safe Card and MazeX and this website, run by Nexeric Games ("I", "me").

What I collect

  • Game data: if you use features like the profile and leaderboard, a username and your points and wins.
  • Emails you send me: your address and message, used only to reply.
  • Ads and device data: the games show ads using Google AdMob, which may process device or advertising identifiers, approximate location, IP address and ad interactions to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Google's privacy policy applies.

How I use it

To run the games, show the leaderboard, fix bugs, answer support emails and show ads. I do not sell your personal data.

Offline play

Playing against the AI does not need an account.

Playing in your browser

If you play in your browser, game progress and settings may be saved in your browser's storage on your own device. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Your choices

  • Ask for your data to be deleted on the Delete my data page.
  • Uninstall a game, or clear this site's data in your browser, to remove data stored on your device.
  • Use your Android settings to reset or limit your advertising ID.

Children

The games are not designed for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child has given me data, email me and I will delete it.

Changes
