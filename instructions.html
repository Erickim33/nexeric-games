How to play

Install the game, then jump in.

Install on Android

  1. On your Android phone, open this site and tap Download on the game you want.
  2. When it finishes, tap the file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser: Settings > Apps > Special access > Install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then Open.
Prefer not to install? Open a game and tap Play in browser.
Only download from this site. APK files from other places may not be safe.
