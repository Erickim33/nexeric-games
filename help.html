Help

Quick answers. Still stuck? Email me.

Can I play without downloading?

Yes. Open a game and tap Play in browser. It works in a modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

The app won't install

Allow installs from your browser (Settings > Apps > Special access > Install unknown apps). Make sure the download finished and you have enough free storage, then try again.

My phone warns me about the file

Android shows this for any app installed outside Google Play. Only install files downloaded from this site.

The download didn't start

Check your connection and try again, or open the site in Chrome. If it still fails, email me.

Why are there ads?

The games are free and ads help support them.

I lost my progress or points

Uninstalling a game can remove data stored on your phone. Email me your username and the game name and I'll look into it.

How do I report a bug?

Email me with the game name, your phone model and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

How do I delete my data?

Use the Delete my data page.
